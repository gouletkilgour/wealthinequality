About this data

Each quarter starts from the Survey of Financial Security (SFS) public use microdata of 2012, 2016, 2019 or 2023. The survey is carried to the quarter in two steps. Each family’s weight grows with the population of its region and the age group of its major income earner. Then six categories of assets and debts (pensions, other financial assets, real estate, other non-financial assets, mortgages and other debts) are scaled to the household sector of the National Balance Sheet Accounts for that quarter.

A quarter between two surveys pools both surveys, each weighted by how close the quarter is to it. Quarters before 2012 Q4 use the 2012 survey alone, and quarters after 2023 Q2 the 2023 survey alone (shaded). Large markers show the survey quarters.

The unit is the economic family, whereas Statistics Canada’s Distributions of Household Economic Accounts use the household, so the shares are not directly comparable. The annual series uses three categories, following the Parliamentary Budget Officer (2020). No adjustment is made for the very wealthy, who are rarely sampled by the survey, so the top 0.1% and 0.01% shares rest on very few records.
